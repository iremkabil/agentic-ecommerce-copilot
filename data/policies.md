## Shipping Policy
- We ship to the United States, the United Kingdom, and the European Union.
- Standard domestic shipping is $4.90 and is free on orders over $40.
- Express domestic shipping is $9.90 (1-2 business day delivery after dispatch).
- International shipping starts at $14.90 and depends on destination and weight.
- Orders are dispatched within 1-2 business days.

## Returns & Refunds Policy
- Unused items in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days for a full refund.
- Return shipping is free for items that arrive defective or incorrect.
- For all other returns, the customer covers return postage.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days of us receiving the return.
- Personalized or engraved items cannot be returned unless they are defective.

## Cancellation & Changes Policy
- Orders can be changed or cancelled within 2 hours of being placed.
- After 2 hours the order may already be in fulfillment and cannot be changed.

## Warranty Policy
- Bamboo desk products carry a 12-month warranty against manufacturing defects.
- The warranty does not cover normal wear, misuse, or accidental damage.

## Privacy Policy
- We only store the details required to fulfill an order (name, contact, shipping address).
- We never sell customer data to third parties.
- Customers can request deletion of their data by contacting support.

## Payment Policy
- This is a demonstration store. No real payment is ever processed and no real
  card details should be entered. Orders reach a draft or confirmed state only.

## Support Scope Policy
- Our assistant helps with products, orders, shipping, returns, and store policies.
- It does not give medical, legal, financial, or safety advice, and it does not
  make health or guaranteed-benefit claims about any product.
