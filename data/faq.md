### How long does shipping take?
Domestic orders are dispatched within 1-2 business days and typically arrive in 3-5 business days. International orders take 7-14 business days depending on the destination.

### How much does shipping cost?
Standard domestic shipping is a flat $4.90, and it is free on orders over $40. Express domestic shipping is $9.90. International shipping starts at $14.90 and varies by destination and order weight.

### Which countries do you ship to?
We currently ship to the United States, the United Kingdom, and the European Union. We do not yet ship to other regions.

### Do you offer gift wrapping?
Yes. Gift wrapping is available at checkout for $3.50 per order and includes a handwritten note of your choice.

### Are your notebooks fountain-pen friendly?
Our 160 gsm dotted and plain A5 notebooks are designed to minimize bleed-through and feathering with most fountain-pen inks. Very wet or broad nibs may still show some show-through.

### What paper weight do you use?
Our A5 softcover notebooks use 160 gsm paper. The B5 hardcover grid notebook uses 120 gsm paper. Planners use 100 gsm paper.

### Can I track my order?
Yes. Once your order ships you will receive an email with a tracking number and a link to follow your delivery.

### How do I change or cancel my order?
You can change or cancel an order within 2 hours of placing it by replying to your confirmation email. After that, the order may already be in fulfillment and cannot be changed.

### Do you restock sold-out items?
Most core items are restocked every 2-4 weeks. You can ask our assistant to note your interest, and we recommend checking the product page for updates.

### What payment methods do you accept?
This is a demonstration store, so no real payments are processed. In a production setting we would accept major credit cards and common digital wallets.

### Do you have a loyalty or rewards program?
Not at this time. This is a portfolio demo, so promotional programs are illustrative only.

### Are your materials sustainable?
Our bamboo desk organizer uses FSC-certified bamboo, and our desk pad uses vegan leather. Packaging is recyclable.

### How do I contact a human agent?
Just ask the assistant to connect you with a person, or mention a complaint or an issue it cannot resolve, and it will create a handoff to a human support agent.
